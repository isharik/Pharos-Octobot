Pharos Builder Base Camp: Bridging TradFi with DeFi to Unlock the Future of RWA, Payments, and Beyond.
Bridging TradFi with DeFi to Unlock the Future of RWA, Payments, and Beyond
The Pharos Builder Base Camp is a global initiative designed to empower developers, startups, and institutions to build the next generation of Real-World Asset (RWA) applications, decentralized payments, and financial innovations on Pharos Network—a modular and full-stack parallel Layer 1 blockchain.
By leveraging the high-performance blockchain architecture, advanced modular stack, and cross-chain interoperability of Pharos, this program bridges the gap between Traditional Finance (TradFi) and Decentralized Finance (DeFi), unlocking new opportunities for financial inclusion, scalability, and market expansion.
Participants will gain hands-on experience, expert mentorship, and access to Pharos’ ecosystem of SPNs (Special Processing Networks), native restaking, and high-speed parallel execution frameworks. Whether you're working on tokenized assets, decentralized payment rails, or cross-chain financial infrastructures, the Builder Base Camp provides the ideal platform to turn ideas into reality.

How Pharos Empowers Builders
Pharos Network is uniquely designed to support complex financial applications and real-world assets with the following features:
Modular & Scalable Infrastructure
L1-Base:
High-performance data availability with hardware acceleration.
L1-Core:
A decentralized, high-throughput blockchain with subsecond finality for real-time transactions.
L1-Extension:
Enables Special Processing Networks (SPNs), cross-SPN interoperability, and native restaking to expand financial applications seamlessly.
High-Speed Parallel Execution & Data Processing
Pharos Virtual Machine (PVM):
Optimized for parallel transaction execution, reducing congestion and latency.
Parallel Merklization & Indexing:
Enables high-speed asset transactions, real-time verification, and seamless integration with external TradFi systems.
Full Life Cycle Asynchronous Pipelining:
Enhances efficiency by processing transactions independently, ensuring uninterrupted performance.
Cross-Chain Interoperability & Financial Ecosystem Integration
Decentralized Data Exchange Protocol:
Synchronizes external financial data sources with Pharos applications to enable real-world financial integrations.
Adaptive Restaking Interaction Protocol:
Facilitates shared security and liquidity circulation, allowing seamless DeFi & TradFi asset movements (e.g., stBTC, stETH, tokenized securities).
SPN-Native Restaking:
Allows developers to create customized financial services leveraging the computing power of Pharos validators.
Builder Base Camp Structure
The Builder Base Camp Program aims to provide technical knowledge, mentorship and support to build and ship projects on Pharos. The program is divided into key phases, starting from onboarding and technical deep dives and going all the way to the pitching projects on the Demo Day. Each phase is designed to ensure that participants not only build but also deploy and scale their innovations on Pharos Network.
The registration process for the Pharos Builder Base Camp will begin from 10th March 2025.
Once the registration is complete, there will be a team formation and onboarding call.
Following the onboarding and team formation, there will be an idea brainstorming session with all the teams participating.
The Builder Base Camp will also have weekly Office-Hours for mentoring and advice.
Why Join THe Pharos Builder Base Camp?
Global Reach
The program welcomes participants from across the world, fostering diverse perspectives and innovative solutions for the financial ecosystem.
High Performance Infrastructure
The parallel execution and specialized processing networks (SPNs) of Pharos ensure real-world financial applications are scalable, efficient, and user-friendly.
Break Boundaries
By integrating TradFi and DeFi, participants will explore the tokenization of RWAs, cross-chain payments, and decentralized finance innovations within the modular and composable ecosystem of Pharos.
Startup & Entrepreneurial Spirit
Builder Base Camp serves as a launchpad for startups and developers, offering a platform to showcase ideas, build prototypes, and gain market traction through grants, incubation, and strategic ecosystem partnerships.
