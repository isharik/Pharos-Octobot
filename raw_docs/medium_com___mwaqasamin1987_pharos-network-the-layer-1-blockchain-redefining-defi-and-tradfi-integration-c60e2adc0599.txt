In the fast-evolving world of blockchain, Pharos Network is emerging as a game-changer. Launched with a vision to bridge traditional finance (TradFi) and decentralized finance (DeFi), this modular, full-stack parallel Layer 1 blockchain is designed for speed, scalability, and real-world adoption. With its testnet live and a mainnet on the horizon, Pharos is poised to reshape how we think about Web3. Let’s dive into what makes Pharos Network stand out.
Lightning-Fast Performance for Mass Adoption
Pharos Network isn’t just another blockchain — it’s built for internet-scale performance. Targeting an impressive 50,000 transactions per second (TPS), Pharos outpaces many existing chains, including Solana and Ethereum’s Layer 2 solutions. With sub-second finality and low fees, it’s ideal for real-time applications like decentralized payments, tokenized Real-World Assets (RWAs), and high-performance decentralized exchanges (DEXs) that rival centralized platforms.
How does it achieve this? Pharos uses a deep-parallel architecture divided into three layers:
L1-Base: Handles data availability and hardware acceleration for robust performance.
L1-Core: Delivers high-throughput transactions with rapid finality.
L1-Extension: Powers Special Processing Networks (SPNs), enabling customized execution for diverse use cases.
This modular design ensures Pharos can scale without sacrificing security or decentralization, making it a strong contender for mass adoption.
A Developer’s Dream: EVM, WASM, and Beyond
Pharos is built with developers in mind. It’s EVM-compatible, allowing seamless migration of Ethereum-based smart contracts, but it doesn’t stop there. By integrating WebAssembly (WASM), Pharos supports a broader range of programming languages and frameworks, from Zero-Knowledge proofs (ZK) to Trusted Execution Environments (TEE) and Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE). This dual-execution model empowers developers to build everything from DeFi protocols to AI-driven applications and decentralized data storage.
The Pharos Builder Base Camp, launched in March 2025, is further fueling innovation. This program offers developers mentorship, tools, and access to SPNs to create cutting-edge applications, particularly in RWAs, payments, and DeFi. With a public Devnet already live, developers can test their ideas on Pharos today.
Restaking and Interoperability: Security Meets Flexibility
Pharos introduces a native restaking protocol, allowing validators to secure multiple SPNs while enhancing liquidity. By integrating with protocols like Babylon and Eigenlayer, Pharos ensures robust security and economic incentives for its ecosystem. Its cross-SPN interoperability enables seamless communication between specialized networks, unlocking new possibilities for complex, multi-chain applications.
Real-World Impact: Bridging TradFi and DeFi
Pharos isn’t just about tech — it’s about solving real problems. Its focus on tokenized RWAs enables assets like real estate, bonds, or commodities to be traded on-chain with unprecedented efficiency. For businesses, Pharos offers instant payment solutions with the reliability of TradFi and the transparency of DeFi. For users, it promises DEXs with CEX-like speed and usability, all while maintaining decentralization.
The Road Ahead
As of May 2025, Pharos Network is in its testnet phase, with a public Devnet inviting developers to explore its capabilities. Backed by an $8M seed round from heavyweights like HackVC and FactionVC, Pharos is gearing up for its mainnet launch in H2 2025. The future looks bright for this ambitious Layer 1, which aims to power the next wave of Web3 innovation.
