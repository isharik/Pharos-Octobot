Introduction to Pharos: Pioneering RealFi
Pharos Network, founded in 2024 and based in the British Virgin Islands, is a programmable financial Layer 1 blockchain tailored for “RealFi” — real-value finance that makes institutional-grade assets accessible to all. Unlike general-purpose chains, Pharos prioritizes unifying traditional (Web2) and decentralized (Web3) systems into a modular on-chain economy. This includes tokenizing RWAs like real estate, bonds, commodities, and infrastructure, enabling fractional ownership and instant settlements. With performance claims of 30,000–50,000 TPS and sub-second finality, Pharos supports real-time applications such as compliant DeFi, stablecoins backed by real collateral, and cross-border payments, all while ensuring transparency and security.
The project’s optimistic vision is rooted in democratizing finance, particularly for underserved regions. By embedding compliance features like zk-KYC (zero-knowledge Know Your Customer) and programmable AML (Anti-Money Laundering), Pharos reduces barriers to entry, fostering trust and adoption. Early metrics, including over 3 billion transactions processed on its testnets, underscore its potential to handle internet-scale operations, positioning it as a positive force for inclusive economic growth.

Technical Architecture: A Parallel Powerhouse
Press enter or click to view image in full size
At its core, Pharos employs a full-stack parallel blockchain design, divided into three layers that operate concurrently for optimal efficiency:
L1-Base
: Ensures robust data availability and hardware acceleration, supporting large-scale operations with tamper-proof storage.
L1-Core
: Delivers high throughput via an asynchronous, leaderless Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT) consensus, with dual-incentive staking (inflation rewards and EIP-1559 fees) to encourage participation.
L1-Extension
: Introduces Special Processing Networks (SPNs) for customizable extensions, enabling sidechains, AI integrations (e.g., ZKML), and native restaking with cross-SPN interoperability.
The Pharos VM (Dora) supports parallel execution, superscalar pipelining, and unified Intermediate Representation for EVM and WASM, making it highly developer-friendly. Integrations with Chainlink’s CCIP (Cross-Chain Interoperability Protocol) and Data Streams (as of October 27, 2025) enhance secure RWA markets and real-time data feeds. Compared to Ethereum’s trie storage, Pharos’s model achieves faster state retrieval and 80% cost reductions, while its modular stack avoids Solana’s single-threaded bottlenecks, promoting greater resilience.
In benchmarks, Pharos’s architecture shines for financial use cases, where sub-second confirmations and near-zero fees enable instant payments — advantages over Sui’s object-centric model, which, while fast, lacks Pharos’s finance-specific compliance layers, or Sei’s trading optimization without broad RWA tooling.
