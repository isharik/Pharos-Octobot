What is the Pharos Network?
The Pharos Network is an emerging L1 blockchain designed to facilitate scalable and efficient transactions, with $PHRS as its native gas token. The project draws its name from the Pharos of Alexandria, the ancient lighthouse that guided ships safely to shore — a metaphor for its mission to illuminate the path toward a robust decentralized future. Backed by prominent investors such as Lightspeed Faction, Hack VC, SNZ, and others,
Pharos raised $8 million in a seed funding round
, as highlighted in a November 2024 announcement on Finsmes.com. The network aims to empower users through a decentralized infrastructure, offering features like governance functions for $PHRS tokens and a suite of tools for developers and participants. While specific tokenomics and the mainnet launch date remain undisclosed, the current testnet phase — Season 1 — is a critical step toward building a thriving ecosystem.
Pharos Testnet Season 1: A Gateway to Rewards
Launched in late June 2025, the Pharos Testnet Season 1 invites participants to engage with the network through a series of tasks, offering the potential to earn $PHRS tokens as rewards. The testnet serves as a proving ground for the network’s technology, allowing users to test its capabilities while incentivizing participation through gamified activities.
